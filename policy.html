Dinu Bakery - Terms, Privacy, Return & Shipping Policies

Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Dinu Bakery! Please review the following Terms & Conditions carefully before using our services.

  • Order Placement: Orders can be placed through our mobile app or website. Ensure all details are accurate to facilitate timely delivery.
  • Order Cancellation: Orders can be canceled within 1 hour of placement by contacting our customer service. Express delivery orders are processed immediately and cannot be canceled.
  • Product Availability: All products are subject to availability. In case of unavailability, we reserve the right to cancel the order and issue a full refund.
  • Pricing: Prices are subject to change without prior notice. The price at the time of order placement is final.
  • User Conduct: Users are expected to use the platform responsibly. Any fraudulent activity or misuse may lead to account suspension.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Below is an outline of how we handle your personal data:

  • Data Collection: We collect personal details such as name, contact, and delivery information to process orders and improve services.
  • Data Usage: Personal information is used to process orders, communicate with customers, and enhance user experience.
  • Data Sharing: We do not sell personal data. We share data with trusted partners like delivery services and payment gateways to fulfill orders.
  • Data Security: We implement security measures to protect your personal information.
  • Your Rights: You can request access to, update, or delete your personal data by contacting us.

Return & Refund Policy

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, we offer a return and refund policy under certain conditions:

  • Return Eligibility: If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery. Perishable items like fruits and bakery goods are non-returnable unless spoiled.
  • Refund Process: Once approved, refunds for online payments are processed within 48 hours and credited to your account within 5-7 working days. For COD orders, refunds will be credited as store credits.
  • Quality Assurance: If any item is of poor quality or defective, please raise an issue within 48 hours of delivery. Claims after that will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

We aim to deliver your orders promptly and efficiently. Here’s how our shipping and delivery works:

  • Delivery Areas: We currently deliver within Thiruvananthapuram. Delivery to other regions will be available soon.
  • Delivery Time: Orders placed before 6 PM are eligible for same-day delivery.
  • Delivery Charges: Free delivery for orders above ₹500. A nominal fee of ₹30 applies to orders below ₹500.
  • Customer Responsibility: Please ensure the accuracy of your delivery details. If an order cannot be delivered due to incorrect information, we may charge a re-delivery fee or cancel the order.
