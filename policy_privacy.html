Dinu Bakery - Privacy Policy

At Dinu Bakery, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information:

  • Data Collection: We collect personal information such as name, contact details, delivery address, and payment information to process orders and improve our services.
  • Data Usage: Your information is used solely for order processing, customer support, and enhancing user experience.
  • Data Sharing: We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with trusted partners like delivery services and payment gateways to fulfill orders.
  • Data Security: We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data.
  • User Rights: You may request access to, correction, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.
