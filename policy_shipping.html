Dinu Bakery - Shipping & Delivery Policy

We aim to deliver your orders promptly and efficiently. Please review our shipping and delivery policy:

  • Delivery Areas: Currently, we deliver to selected pin codes in Thiruvananthapuram.
  • Delivery Time: Orders placed before 6 PM are eligible for same-day delivery.
  • Delivery Charges: Delivery is free for orders above ₹500. A fee of ₹30 applies to orders below this amount.
  • Customer Responsibility: Ensure the delivery address and contact details are accurate. Failure to receive the order due to incorrect information or unavailability may void our "Fresh or Free" commitment.
